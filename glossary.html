Glossary

Abbreviations explained

The abbreviations and terms used on this site, in plain words.

Terms

AI and risk

AI
Artificial intelligence.
LLM
Large language model. The kind of AI model behind chatbots, which reads and writes text.
AI agent
AI that can take actions in other systems on its own, not only answer questions.
Shadow AI
AI tools that people use without approval or without anyone knowing.
AAIR
Advanced in AI Risk. ISACA certification for managing AI risk.
CRISC
Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control. ISACA certification for IT risk management.
ISACA
Global professional association for IT governance, risk, audit and security.

Governance, audit and roles

GRC
Governance, risk and compliance.
SoD
Segregation of duties. No single person can carry out a critical process from start to end alone.
ITGC
IT general controls. Basic controls over IT systems, such as access and change management, that auditors test.
SOX
Sarbanes-Oxley Act. US law that requires controls over financial reporting.
UAT
User acceptance testing. Users test a change before it goes live.
PO
Product owner. Decides what a development team builds.
CISO
Chief Information Security Officer.
HR / HRIS
Human resources, and the HR information system that supports it.

Privacy and EU law

EU AI Act
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. The EU law on artificial intelligence.
Digital Omnibus
Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. Changed the AI Act, including later dates for high-risk rules.
Provider / deployer
AI Act roles. The provider builds or brands an AI system. The deployer uses it.
Annex III
The AI Act list of high-risk areas, such as recruitment and credit.
Art.
Article of a regulation, for example AI Act Art. 50.
GDPR
General Data Protection Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2016/679. The EU law on personal data.
DPO
Data protection officer. Advises on and monitors how personal data is handled.
DPIA
Data protection impact assessment. A GDPR check required before high-risk processing of personal data.
EUR-Lex
The official online database of EU law.

Standards and frameworks

ISO / IEC
International Organization for Standardization and International Electrotechnical Commission. They publish international standards, some jointly.
ISO/IEC 42001
Standard for an AI management system.
ISO/IEC 27001
Standard for an information security management system.
ISO/IEC 20000-1
Standard for IT service management.
ISO 21502
Guidance on project management.
Clause and control numbers
6.1.2 is a clause of a standard. A.5.15 is a control in its Annex A.
NIST AI RMF
US National Institute of Standards and Technology AI Risk Management Framework. Four functions: Govern, Map, Measure, Manage.
COBIT
ISACA framework for governance and management of IT. Objectives have codes such as BAI07 (IT change acceptance), DSS05 (Security services) and DSS06 (Business process controls).
ITIL
Framework of good practice for IT service management, such as problem and knowledge management.
COSO
Internal control framework widely used for financial and operational controls.

Systems and tools

IAM
Identity and access management.
SSO
Single sign-on. One login gives access to several systems.
ERP
Enterprise resource planning. Core business system for finance, purchasing and similar processes.
SAP
A widely used ERP system.
API
Application programming interface. How systems connect to each other.
MCP
Model Context Protocol. A standard way to connect an AI model to tools and data.
HTML
The language web pages are written in.
UX
User experience.
