Glossary

Terms and abbreviations

The abbreviations and terms used on this site. Where a term is defined in a law or standard, the definition follows it and the source is shown.

Terms

AI terms

AI system
A machine-based system that operates with some autonomy and, from its input, infers how to generate outputs such as predictions, content, recommendations or decisions.EU AI Act Art. 3(1)
AI management system
The policies, objectives and processes an organisation uses for the responsible development, provision or use of AI systems.ISO/IEC 42001
Human oversight
People can monitor an AI system, understand its output, and intervene or stop it.EU AI Act Art. 14
Human-in-the-loop
A person reviews and approves the AI output before it is used.
LLM
Large language model. An AI model trained on large amounts of text that can read and generate text.
AI agent
An AI system that can take actions in other systems on its own.
Shadow AI
AI tools used without approval or without the organisation knowing.

EU AI Act

EU AI Act
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, the EU law on artificial intelligence.
Digital Omnibus
Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. Amends the AI Act, including later dates for high-risk obligations.
Provider
Develops an AI system, or has one developed, and places it on the market or puts it into service under its own name or trademark.EU AI Act Art. 3(3)
Deployer
Uses an AI system under its authority, except for personal, non-professional use.EU AI Act Art. 3(4)
Prohibited practice
An AI practice that is banned in the EU, such as social scoring or emotion recognition at work.EU AI Act Art. 5
High-risk AI system
An AI system used in an area listed in Annex III, or as a safety component of a regulated product, with extra obligations.EU AI Act Art. 6
Annex III
The list of high-risk areas, such as employment, education, credit and access to essential services.EU AI Act
Art.
Article of a regulation, for example AI Act Art. 50.

Privacy (GDPR)

GDPR
General Data Protection Regulation, Regulation (EU) 2016/679.
Personal data
Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.GDPR Art. 4(1)
Special category data
Personal data revealing, for example, health, biometric data, ethnic origin, religion or political opinions, with stricter rules.GDPR Art. 9
Processor
A party that processes personal data on behalf of the controller.GDPR Art. 4(8)
Record of processing activities
The register an organisation keeps of how and why it processes personal data.GDPR Art. 30
DPO
Data protection officer. Informs, advises and monitors compliance with data protection rules.GDPR Art. 37–39
DPIA
Data protection impact assessment. Required before processing that is likely to result in a high risk to people.GDPR Art. 35
EUR-Lex
The official online database of EU law.

Governance, risk and control

GRC
Governance, risk and compliance.
Asset inventory
A list of information and other assets, each with an identified owner.ISO/IEC 27001 A.5.9
Asset owner
The person or role accountable for an asset, including its protection and access.ISO/IEC 27001 A.5.9
SoD
Segregation of duties. Conflicting duties are split between different people to reduce the risk of fraud and error.ISO/IEC 27001 A.5.3
Change management
Changes to systems follow a defined process of assessment, approval, testing and release.ISO/IEC 27001 A.8.32
ITGC
IT general controls. Controls over IT systems, such as access and change management, that support reliable financial reporting.
SOX
Sarbanes-Oxley Act. US law requiring internal controls over financial reporting.
UAT
User acceptance testing. Users test a change before it goes live.
Release gate
A check a change must pass before it can go live.

Roles and agile terms

PO
Product owner. Responsible for the product backlog and what the team builds.Scrum Guide
Scrum master
Helps the Scrum team follow Scrum and remove blockers.Scrum Guide
Refinement
Backlog refinement: breaking down and detailing upcoming work.Scrum Guide
User story
A short description of a piece of work from the user's point of view.
Definition of Done
The shared description of when work is complete and can be released.Scrum Guide
Epic
A large piece of work, split into smaller tickets.
SME
Subject matter expert.
CISO
Chief Information Security Officer.
HR / HRIS
Human resources, and the HR information system that supports it.

Standards and frameworks

ISO / IEC
International Organization for Standardization and International Electrotechnical Commission. They publish international standards, some jointly.
ISO/IEC 42001
Requirements for an AI management system.
ISO/IEC 27001
Requirements for an information security management system.
ISO/IEC 20000-1
Requirements for a service management system.
ISO 21502
Guidance on project management.
Clause / Annex A control
Clauses (for example 6.1.2) are the requirements of a standard. Annex A controls (for example A.5.15) are the reference controls.
NIST AI RMF
AI Risk Management Framework of the US National Institute of Standards and Technology. Four functions: Govern, Map, Measure and Manage.
COBIT
ISACA framework for the governance and management of enterprise IT. Objectives have codes such as BAI07 (Managed IT Change Acceptance and Transitioning), DSS05 (Managed Security Services) and DSS06 (Managed Business Process Controls).
COSO
Internal Control, Integrated Framework, widely used for financial and operational controls.
ISACA
Global professional association for IT governance, risk, audit and security.
AAIR
Advanced in AI Risk. ISACA certification for AI risk management.
CRISC
Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control. ISACA certification for IT risk management.

Systems and tools

IAM
Identity and access management.
SSO
Single sign-on. One login gives access to several systems.
Service account
A non-human account used by a system or application.
ERP
Enterprise resource planning. Core business system for finance, purchasing and similar processes.
SAP
A widely used ERP system.
API
Application programming interface. How systems connect to each other.
MCP
Model Context Protocol. An open standard for connecting AI models to tools and data.
HTML
The language web pages are written in.
UX
User experience.
